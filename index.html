The Project

I created a campaign for a proposed Twenty One Pilots show at Ohio Stadium. I designed posters featuring Tyler, Josh, and the stadium, plus a ticket and an exclusive Breach vinyl with a custom sleeve, disc, and insert. I made all the pieces in Adobe Illustrator.

This is a fan-made concept, not a real concert announcement. I wanted the campaign to feel like something fans might see around Columbus or online. The posters use the same red, black, and rough print style so they feel connected, while each one focuses on a different part of the imagined event.

The Idea

As a longtime fan, I created this project to hype up other fans and imagine what a hometown stadium show could feel like. FROM HERE is a tribute to the band, their story, and the Skeleton Clique—a celebration of the people who make this music meaningful.

I wanted the project to feel big enough for a stadium, but still personal. The posters show the event from different angles: the band, the venue, and the energy of a live performance. Together, they make the imagined show feel like one complete campaign.
