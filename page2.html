The Project Campaign Pieces Visual Direction

The Vinyl

The visual system combines Twenty One Pilots’ red and black with raw, textured imagery, nostalgic print treatments, and hand-drawn details. The package connects the imagined show to the band’s music and gives the campaign a physical piece fans could keep.

The Insert

A personal insert comes with the vinyl. It includes original artwork and a handwritten message from me to the fans—a reminder of the connection, support, and people who keep this community alive.

Tickets

Custom ticket designs bring the campaign’s visual style into a collectible fan experience.

Social Rollout

The campaign lives on social media, reusing the poster visuals to build excitement for the show and vinyl release. A simple, consistent layout keeps the focus on the artwork and message.
